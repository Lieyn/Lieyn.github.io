## 4. The mechanism: monitoring, not chess knowledge

<!--
The "why." Transfer isn't chess content migrating to unrelated domains — that
would be implausible on its face. It's that explicit heuristic instruction
trains metacognitive monitoring: catching your own fast pattern-match before
you commit to it, checking the assumption you didn't know you'd made.

Kazemi, Yektayar & Abad (2012): math g = 0.686, cognitive/metacognitive
g = 0.819.

THEN THE PREEMPT: CCSCSL independently codes Kazemi as Tier I. State this
explicitly. It kills the obvious objection that you picked a weak study
because it said what you wanted.
-->
